You can either:
1. Drag and drop / click 'Upload Plan' directly on the web page at `/projects/aurum-villas` under Floor Plans (saved instantly to localStorage), OR
2. Place your image files here and register their paths in `DEFAULT_SLOT_IMAGES` inside `lib/slots.ts`.

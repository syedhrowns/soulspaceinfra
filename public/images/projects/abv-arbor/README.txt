ABV ARBOR - IMAGE ASSETS DIRECTORY
==================================

Slot Mappings:
- abvarbor_img_16 (alias: abvarbor_img_terrace): Terrace Garden | Kids Play Area
  Suggested format: WebP / PNG / JPG
  Recommended aspect: Landscape (16:10 or 16:9)

You can either:
1. Drag and drop / click 'Upload Image' directly on the web page in your browser (saved instantly to localStorage), OR
2. Place your image here (e.g. `public/images/projects/abv-arbor/terrace-garden.webp`) and update `DEFAULT_SLOT_IMAGES` in `lib/slots.ts` with `/images/projects/abv-arbor/terrace-garden.webp`.
